## One workspace, four practical tools


| Your task | Velune tool |
| --- | --- |
| Get HYPE to pay network fees | **Refuel** — make a small source-chain payment and receive gas from the destination reserve. |
| Distribute tokens to a community | **Batch Send** — import a list, review totals and authorize batches in your wallet. |
| Keep tokens or supported liquidity locked | **Locker** — choose an unlock date or a tranche schedule. |
| Release an allocation over time | **Vesting** — configure a cliff and a linear or periodic schedule. |


Explore shared lock and vesting pages, claim available tokens and track your activity in **My Velune**.


<details>
<summary><strong>How it works</strong></summary>


1. Choose your task and asset.
2. Review recipients, amounts, timing and the displayed fees.
3. Confirm the required transactions in your own wallet.
4. Follow receipts, schedules and claims in the interface.


Refuel uses a dedicated operator and a funded HYPE reserve. It depends on available liquidity and supported source chains.
</details>
